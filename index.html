About

Fire & Rescue Background

Before construction, John spent 14 years as a firefighter/EMT with Frederick County Fire and Rescue in Maryland (2004–2018) — structural firefighting, technical rope rescue, and emergency medical response. After relocating to Tennessee, his Pro Board–accredited certifications were formally recognized by the Tennessee Commission on Firefighting via reciprocity in 2020, affiliated with Williamson County Fire Rescue. That background in life-safety systems and high-pressure teamwork is part of what shows up in the way IKON Electric ran: careful, code-compliant, and accountable.
